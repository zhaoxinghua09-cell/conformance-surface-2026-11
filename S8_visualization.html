1 Evidence-grading vocabulary (V / S / M / L / P)

Carried through both contributions as the check input language — never as a verdict. Adapted from a GRADE-style four-level scheme (BMJ 2008), not affiliated with the GRADE Working Group.

V
Verified
Cross-verified against independent sources
Consequence: eligible for automated trust
S
Single-source
One source only, not yet cross-checked
Consequence: eligible with human-in-loop
M
Model-derived
Inferred / model-produced, not externally attested
Consequence: conditional, flagged
L
Low / unverified
Missing or contradictory evidence
Consequence: quarantined
P
Prohibited / poisoned
Known-bad or tamper-indicated
Consequence: blocked

Authorization states carried alongside the grade: verified · unresolved · rejected · quarantined

2 The four checks (W3C s-agent-comm deliverable)

Each check answers one relying-party question: given an agent identifier, what must I be able to verify, and what evidence makes it pass or fail? Click a check to expand its evidence items.

V verified S single-source M model-derived L low/unverified P prohibited
Check 1 Delegation-chain verifiability Can a relying party verify the ordered delegation chain, root → agent?
V
Every link resolves to a verifiable delegator→delegate binding with a signed scope statement (delegator, delegate, capability, not-before, not-after).
S
The chain resolves but one or more links rely on a single (un-cross-checked) source.
M
The chain is asserted by the agent itself with no independent delegator attestation.
L
One or more links missing, expired, or unverifiable.
P
A link is revoked yet still presented as active (tamper-indicated).
PASS: every link V or S, none L or PFAIL: any link L or P
Seam: reads the group's delegation vocabulary (AgentIDL constrainable delegation) and identity vocabulary (Agent Identity Registry). Verifies the chain only — does not decide whether the delegation should have been granted.
Check 2 Execution-record integrity Is the agent's semantic log present, tamper-evident, attributable?
V
The log is hash-chained, each entry signed by the agent key, and entries are contiguous (no gaps).
S
The log is complete but one segment is signed by an intermediate delegate rather than the agent key.
M
The log is reconstructed by a model/inference from side channels, not from signed records.
L
Log entries missing or unsigned.
P
A hash-chain break or a forged signature detected.
PASS: V or S across the audited window, none L or PFAIL: any L or P
Seam: reads the group's semantic logs vocabulary. This is the execution-record half of the 30-Oct promise.
Check 3 Authorization binding Does the gate produce a reproducible admit/deny verdict from enumerable inputs?
V
The gate's input fields (delegation state, evidence grade, requested action) are enumerated, and the verdict is a pure function of those inputs.
S
Inputs enumerated, verdict reproducible, but one input (e.g. revocation state) read from a single source.
M
Verdict produced by a model with no enumerable input contract.
L
Verdict inputs not documented; the gate is opaque.
P
The gate admits an action for which a P-graded input exists (contradiction).
PASS: V or SFAIL: L or P
Seam: reads the group's capability description and conformance definitions vocabulary. Marks the gate's input read-point, never the verdict itself.
Check 4 Accountability continuity (revocation / rollback) After revocation/rollback, does the record show coherent before/after with no orphans?
V
Revocation/rollback state propagates to the execution record; the record shows the state transition with a timestamp and the revoking authority.
S
Transition recorded but the revoking authority attested by a single source.
M
Transition inferred, not recorded in the signed log.
L
No record of the transition; orphaned authorizations may persist.
P
An authorization remains effective after an explicit revocation (orphan detected).
PASS: V or S, none L or PFAIL: any L or P
Seam: reads the group's intent modelling and conformance definitions vocabulary; closes the loop between Check 1 (delegation) and Check 2 (execution record).
Honest scope note: Checks 3–4 read the capability-description / intent-modelling / conformance-definitions vocabulary that the 30-Oct message itself enumerated as in-scope (the group's nine scope areas). They do not reach outside that vocabulary and do not propose new normative text.
